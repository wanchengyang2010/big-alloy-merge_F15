Big Alloy Merge (合成大YK) is a Suika-style physics merge game built with Python and pygame-ce. Drop elemental balls into a container, collide same-tier pairs to fuse them into higher-tier elements, and chain your way up through 17 unique elements to reach the ultimate prize — the legendary 钇钾 🪙. With a 480 FPS physics engine, rotational collision mechanics, and six distinct game modes, every session is a fresh challenge of strategy and physics intuition.

## Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from zero to playing your first game.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download">
    Visit the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/wanchengyang2010/big-alloy-merge/releases) and download the latest release for your platform. Windows users should grab `Big_Alloy_Merge_Setup.exe`; Linux users should download the `.AppImage` file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    On Windows, run the NSIS installer and follow the on-screen wizard — it creates desktop and Start Menu shortcuts automatically. On Linux, mark the AppImage executable with `chmod +x` and run it directly. No additional runtimes required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Open Big Alloy Merge from your desktop shortcut or application menu. The splash screen loads all assets, then takes you to the mode selection screen. Press **B** to jump straight into Lite mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    Drag your mouse inside the container and release to drop a ball. When two balls of the same tier touch, they fuse into the next tier. Keep stacking, triggering chain reactions, and fusing upward — your goal is to create 钇钾 🪙.
  </Step>
</Steps>
