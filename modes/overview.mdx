Big Alloy Merge ships with six distinct game modes, each tuned for a different kind of experience. Whether you're a first-time player looking for a gentle introduction, a physics tinkerer who wants to adjust every variable in real time, or someone who wants to watch an AI bot chain-react its way through a pile of balls, there's a mode for you. The mode select screen greets you every time you launch the game — press the corresponding key or click the card to jump straight in.

## Mode Comparison

| Mode | Key | Elements | Container Width | Dynamic Drop | Score Saved | Description |
|------|-----|----------|-----------------|:------------:|:-----------:|-------------|
| Lite | `B` | 12 | 480 ref | ❌ | ✅ | Classic difficulty, recommended for new players |
| Full | `A` | 17 | 540 ref | ✅ | ✅ | Full 17-element chain, harder |
| Debug | `C` | 12 | 480 ref | ❌ | ❌ | Requires password 3919, basic param panel |
| Full Debug | (card) | 17 | 540 ref | ✅ | ❌ | Requires password 3919, full real-time panel |
| Demo | `D` | 12 | 480 ref | ❌ | ❌ | AI autoplay (SmartBot / DumbBot / DemoBot) |
| Q Self | `Q` | 12 | 480 ref | ❌ | ✅ | Custom images/names/messages via q_custom.json |

<Tip>
  Start with **Lite mode** if you're new to Big Alloy Merge. Its 12-element chain and narrower container give you a comfortable introduction to the merge mechanics before you tackle the full 17-element experience.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Debug** and **Full Debug** modes are password-protected. Type `3919` at the mode select screen, then choose your debug variant. Scores recorded in either debug mode are not saved to your history.
</Note>
