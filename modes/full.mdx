Full mode is Big Alloy Merge at its most expansive. You play with all 17 elements — from the diminutive 西西 🕴️ at tier 0 all the way to the ultimate 钇钾 🪙 at tier 16 — inside a wider 540-ref container. As your merges climb higher, the dynamic drop system gradually introduces larger and rarer balls into your drop pool, rewarding skillful play with even more powerful pieces to place. Every session score counts toward your history, just like Lite mode.

<Warning>
  Full mode is significantly harder than Lite. The overflow line sits higher in the container (179 ref vs. 135 ref), the element chain is five tiers longer, and the dynamic drop system can flood your stack with large balls if you're not ready for them. Get comfortable with Lite mode before making the jump.
</Warning>

## What Makes Full Mode Unique

- **17-element chain** — the complete fusion ladder from 西西 (tier 0) to 钇钾 (tier 16), including the v2.3 additions 春宇 ♾️ (tier 14) and the reworked tier 0.
- **Wider container (540 ref)** — gives you more horizontal room, but the extra space also means larger balls have more room to pile up dangerously.
- **Dynamic drop system** — merging into higher tiers progressively unlocks bigger ball types for dropping, adding a layer of strategic decision-making unique to Full mode.
- **Magnetic attraction** — same-tier balls exert a slight attractive force on each other, nudging nearby pairs together to help trigger merges.
- **Score recorded** — your session score is saved to history after every game.

## Dynamic Drop System

The dynamic drop system (introduced in v2.3.0.0) ties your drop pool directly to your merge progress. As you push further up the tier ladder, new and larger balls become available to drop — but they arrive with rate limits to keep the game balanced.

### How It Works

Whenever you merge two balls of tier N, the game unlocks tier N−2 as a possible drop:

| Merge Achievement | Drop Tier Unlocked | Rate Limit |
|-------------------|--------------------|------------|
| Merge tier 8 (考拉) | Tier 6 (家畜) drops available | Max 1 per 5 balls dropped |
| Merge tier 10 (阳阳) | Tier 7 (猴子) & tier 8 (考拉) drops available | Max 1 per 8 balls dropped |
| Merge tier N | Tier N−2 drops available | Tier N−3: 1 per 5 balls; Tier N−2: 1 per 8 balls |

### Rate Limits Explained

Two rate limits apply simultaneously once higher tiers are unlocked:

- **Tier N−3 cap** — at most 1 occurrence per every 5 balls dropped.
- **Tier N−2 cap** — at most 1 occurrence per every 8 balls dropped (tighter, because this tier is closer to your frontier).

This prevents the drop pool from becoming dominated by large balls too quickly, while still rewarding skilled merging with occasional powerful drops.

## Container Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Container width | 540 ref |
| Overflow line | 179 ref from the top |
| Drop delay | 0.25 s between drops |
| Overflow timeout | 3.0 s |

## How to Start

Press **`A`** on the mode select screen to launch Full mode. You can also click the **Full** card.

## Magnetic Attraction

In Full mode, same-tier balls that come within range of each other exert a gentle attractive force. This helps nudge close-but-not-quite-touching pairs into contact so they merge naturally, reducing the frustration of near-misses on small high-tier balls buried in the stack.
